# Copyright / sources audit · 2026-11-18

## Excluded outright
- All player portraits, event photographs, third-party images and any ambiguous photo licensing.
- Official university/department/event logos, copyrighted identity artwork, sponsor artwork, official mascots.
- Official member diary titles/bodies, long excerpts, translations of unpublished articles, full news text and third-party charts.
- `wsd_community_submissions.image_path` and image storage: intentionally never fetched or rendered.
- `wsd_community_editorial.body`, `wsd_event_news.summary`: intentionally not migrated, as the provenance and republication rights of prose are not established.

## Included
- Numerical race results, athlete name (original Japanese), date, event/leg, time, rank and original source URL, as fact fields.
- PB fields from original 2026 roster (clearly marked as not yet individually verified) and World Athletics ranking facts with source and dated snapshot.
- New concise explanatory text, not quoted from any news page, plus CSS-only shapes, custom favicon and original layout.
- Click-through links to original sources without inline image, logo or copied article text.

## To verify before announcing “officially confirmed”
- Each PB result should have a meet/date and a direct official result citation.
- Rankings should specify whether season list, Japanese national student list, Japanese-national student all-time list, world performance list or World Athletics *points* ranking.
- No mixed-category inferred ranking, or inferred world PB rank.
- All 1920–2026 competition records originate in the previous project; the project should retain an editorial verification queue, especially when courses changed.

## Permission and attribution
Source links are citations, **not** licenses to copy media. If permission is ambiguous, do not import it. Requests to remove content should be processed as factual-error or copyright reports in a future contact workflow. Do not copy Google OAuth profile photos.
